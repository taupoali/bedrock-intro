RunITAnywhere is offered under three pricing plans: Starter, Professional, and Enterprise.

The Starter plan supports a single cloud account and up to 10 git repos and includes basic cost visibility, integrated app deployment and monthly summary reports.

The Professional plan supports up to 100 cloud accounts, 1000 git repos and includes realtime cost analysis, integrated app deployment, optimization recommendations, and email alerts.

The Enterprise plan supports unlimited cloud accounts, unlimited git repos and and includes custom reporting, role-based access control, SAML & OpenID authentication,and priority support.

Pricing is based on the number of cloud accounts connected to the platform.
